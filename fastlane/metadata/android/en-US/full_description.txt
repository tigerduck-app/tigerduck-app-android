TigerDuck is an open-source campus assistant for students at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST).

It brings scattered campus services together in one app:

- Assignments: synced automatically from Moodle, with deadline reminders and an ongoing notification for what's due next
- Class schedule: synced straight from the course selection system, with an interactive timeline that shows where your next class is
- Grades: semester and cumulative GPA, rankings and every course's grade, with a chart of how your grades trend
- Calendar: school events and Moodle deadlines together in one month view
- Announcements: notices from every office and center on one page, sorted into categories you can subscribe to
- Library (experimental): open your library entry QR code instantly, or just turn your phone face down
- TigerSync: keep assignments and your class schedule in sync across Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac, choosing item by item what syncs (the F-Droid version keeps to "Essential information")
- Personalization: 65 languages and regional variants, your own tabs, home sections and theme color, and shortened course and classroom names

On Wear OS (Google Play version only):

- A watch app that shows the class you're in or the next one coming up, plus today's classes and course details
- A tile: swipe to it on your watch to see your current or next class, with the course name, classroom, instructor and start or end time
- A watch face complication: add it to your watch face to see the class in progress, or your next class and when it starts
- Your library entry QR code right on your wrist
- Your schedule, language and theme color sync from your phone automatically

The Wear OS app needs Wear OS 3 or later and a paired phone with the Google Play version of TigerDuck.

Built by students, for students.
